How Banner Ads Work
  • Admin sets price per page + size + duration. Different combos have different rates.
  • Empty slots show “Advertise Here” — clicking takes you here to place an ad.
  • Image is uploaded and saved as base64 (no external hosting needed).
  • After payment, your ad is pending review. Admin approves to make it live.
  • Once active, ad runs until expiry date (start + duration). Clicks & impressions tracked.
  • Rejected ads are refunded automatically.
