How to Play

Rules & controls

Quick start

Play. Win. Earn.

Matches are played in landscape. Eliminate the enemy team to reach the score target before the clock runs out.

Match Flow

1

Choose a match type

1v1 Duel, 2v2 Squad Duel or 4v4 Team Battle, plus a game mode.

2

Pick your map

Some maps stay locked until you reach the required level.

3

Set your loadout

Primary, secondary, grenade and melee. Buy new gear in the shops.

4

Fight

First team to the score target wins. Timer runs out? Highest score takes it.

5

Claim rewards

Coins and XP land in your game wallet. Coins buy characters and weapons.
